## Fixed findings
- Production engine (`KAPYorumEngine`) was scrubbed of the `_override_readiness_for_testing` bypass method, making it fail-closed definitively. Testing overrides are handled via strict subclassing (`TestEngine`).
- Implemented `validate_error_category_invariant` in `RequestMetadata` via Pydantic `model_validator` to actively reject states where `UNAVAILABLE`, `INVALID_RESPONSE`, or failing `PARTIAL` lack an error category, or where `SUCCESS`/`EMPTY_CONFIRMED` carry one.
- Corrected the `SCHEMA_MISMATCH` catch-all by explicitly tracking `DUPLICATE_IDENTIFIER`, `MISSING_IDENTIFIER`, and `MULTIPLE_ERRORS` inside `KAPClient`.
- Implemented explicit datetime boundary checking leveraging `v.tzinfo.utcoffset(v) is not None` to prevent ambiguous naive datetimes from slipping through validation.
